About Us

Who We Are

Kelibia, Tunisia

Last Bullet Studio is an independent game development and 3D production studio based in Tunisia.

We combine original game development with professional outsourcing services, working on our own intellectual properties while collaborating with other companies in the gaming industry.

Where we are going

Our Vision

We want Last Bullet Studio to stand for two things: original games worth playing, and production art other studios can rely on.

Each side feeds the other. Building our own games keeps us honest about what works in an engine; working with partner studios keeps our craft sharp and our pipeline professional.
